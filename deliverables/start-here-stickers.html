Adding to GoodNotes

Two ways to use your stickers in GoodNotes. Method 01 saves them to your library for repeated use across any notebook.

Method 01 / Sticker Library

  1. Save the PNG files to your iPad Files app
  2. Open GoodNotes and open any notebook
  3. Tap the sticker icon in the toolbar
  4. Tap + and select "New Collection"
  5. Name it, then tap "Add Images"
  6. Select your stickers from Files

Method 02 / Insert as Image

  1. Save the PNG files to your iPad Files app
  2. Open your GoodNotes notebook
  3. Tap the Insert icon in the toolbar
  4. Choose "Image" and select your sticker
  5. Resize and position it on the page
Good to know: Your sticker files are PNGs with transparent backgrounds. They layer cleanly on top of any page color. Method 01 is recommended if you plan to use these often since it saves them permanently to your library.
