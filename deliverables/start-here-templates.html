Opening in GoodNotes

Two ways to get your file into GoodNotes. Pick whichever fits the way you usually work.

Method 01 / Direct Import

  1. Save the PDF to your iPad
  2. Open GoodNotes and tap the + icon
  3. Select "Import" and choose your file
  4. GoodNotes creates a new notebook from the PDF

Method 02 / Files App

  1. Save the PDF to your iPad Files app
  2. Open the Files app and locate your file
  3. Long-press the file and tap "Share"
  4. Select GoodNotes from the share sheet
Using Notability or another PDF app? The process is nearly identical. Import or share the PDF directly into your app of choice.
