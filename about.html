My Story

I spent years in corporate operations. Then I built something that actually fits.

Hey, I'm Tiara. Everyone calls me T. I'm a Certified Project Manager, business operations specialist, and web designer, but I also create digital planning products, study astrology, and love to write.

Before I founded The Intentional Tea, I spent just under seven years at one of the largest tech companies in the world. When I was laid off in 2024, it turned out to be the opening I didn't know I needed. That season gave me room to get intentional about my own life first, then build the professional life I actually wanted from there.

I built this brand to bring together everything I know: the operational systems and structures I used to run enterprise programs, and the personal tools I used to put my own life back together. One side is for business owners who need their backend to match how big they're thinking. The other is for anyone building a personal practice with real intention behind it.

The Philosophy

Three things that guide everything here.

The worldview behind the products, the services, and the brand.

01

Alignment over effort

Build structure that matches how you actually function.

Working harder inside a system that doesn't fit you is just friction with extra steps. The goal is always to build something that works with your actual patterns, not against them.

02

Systems create freedom

The right infrastructure is what gives you room to breathe.

Structure is not constraint. A properly built operational backbone is what lets you step back, delegate, and scale without everything collapsing the moment you do.

03

One life, two sides

Your business and your life are not separate problems.

You cannot build a sustainable business while ignoring the life around it. That's why this brand holds both: operational tools for the business and intentional tools for the person running it.
