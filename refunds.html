Refund Policy

Last updated 26 September 2026

Short version: digital downloads are final once delivered, because they cannot be returned. If something is genuinely wrong, it gets put right. Email theintentionaltea@gmail.com.

Digital products

All sales are final once the file has been delivered. A download cannot be given back, which is why this is the standard across digital shops.

Every listing says what is included, how many pages, what format, and what you need to open it. Please read that before buying, and ask first if anything is unclear.

When a refund will be given

Being final does not mean being unreasonable. A refund or replacement will be issued if:

  • The file is corrupted, will not open, or is materially different from what the listing described.
  • You were charged twice for the same item.
  • You bought the same product twice by mistake. Tell me within 30 days.
  • A personalised product, such as the Astrology Report, has not yet been started.

Get in touch within 30 days of purchase and describe what went wrong. A fix comes first, and a working file usually solves it. If it cannot be fixed, the money comes back.

When it will not

  • You changed your mind after downloading.
  • You did not read what was included.
  • You do not have the software to open it. Every listing states the format.
  • You did not get the results you hoped for. These are tools, not outcomes.

Bought through Etsy

Purchases made on Etsy follow Etsy's own process, because they handle the payment. Start there, but do email as well and it will be sorted either way.

Services

Deposits hold your place in the schedule and are non-refundable once work has started, because the time has been reserved and the early work is already done. If a project is cancelled part way, you are billed for work completed to that point and the rest is returned.

How long it takes

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method, usually within 5 to 10 business days depending on your bank.
