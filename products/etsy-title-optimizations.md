## SEO NOTES

- All titles lead with the search term (Digital Planner, Business Workbook, etc.) not the brand name
- GoodNotes + Notability called out in digital planner titles — these are the top Etsy search terms
- "Digital" + "PDF" + "Undated" + "Hyperlinked" are high-volume for planner searches
- Sticker titles include "PNG" and "iPad" per top search behavior
- Astrology Report leads with "Personalized" — that's what buyers search when they want a reading, not a template
- Do NOT add "The Intentional Tea" to any title — brand names hurt Etsy ranking, they're not keywords buyers use
