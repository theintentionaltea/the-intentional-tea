# Pricing Page (High-Conversion)

Design a pricing page that helps visitors **choose** and feel good about it. Your job is not to "show prices." Your job is to **reduce uncertainty**.

---

## Before You Design/Write

1. **Offer + audience** — what are you selling, who is it for, what's the value metric?
2. **Plans** — names, prices, limits (3-6 that matter), what's the upgrade trigger?
3. **Objections + risk** — top 3 reasons people don't buy; can you offer free trial, guarantee, or demo?
4. **Proof** — testimonials, results, metrics
5. **Traffic context** — where are visitors coming from?

---

## Core Structure

### Above the Fold (Must)
- Clear value headline (what outcome, for who)
- Monthly/annual toggle with annual savings callout
- 3-plan pricing table (most common) or 2-plan (simple product)
- Primary CTA per plan (consistent verbs)

### Below the Fold (High Leverage)
- Plan comparison (feature matrix or "what you get" bullets)
- FAQ (objection handling)
- Social proof near decision points
- Final CTA + contact option

---

## Layout Types

### A) Classic 3-Card
Best when: you have 3 natural tiers  
Rules: 1 plan labeled **Recommended**, show "most popular" without yelling

### B) Value Metric Slider
Best when: pricing scales with usage (seats, credits, events)  
Rules: keep math obvious, safe default at median customer

### C) "Pick Your Path" (Two Columns)
Best when: different audiences (Individuals vs Teams)  
Rules: separate by persona first, then price

---

## High-Conversion Strategies

### 1. Make the Decision Easy
- 3 plans max
- One recommended plan
- Bullets describe outcomes, not internal features

### 2. Anchor Value
- Annual toggle with "Save X%"
- Show starting price only if pricing is genuinely variable

### 3. Reduce Risk
Choose at least one:
- Free trial
- Money-back guarantee
- "Talk to us" with clear response time promise

### 4. Handle Objections Before They Bounce

Most effective FAQ topics:
- "Can I cancel anytime?"
- "What format does this come in?"
- "Do you offer refunds?"
- "Is this for beginners or advanced?"
- "What do I need to use this?"

### 5. Provide a Readable Comparison
- Group by categories, not a giant flat list
- Highlight what changes at each tier

## Copywriting Templates

### Headlines
- "{Outcome} for {audience} — without {pain}"
- "Plans that scale from {starting point} to {growth}"

### Plan Descriptions (2 lines)
- Who it's for
- What it unlocks

### CTA Buttons
Use verbs that match the motion:
- "Get the Planner" (purchase)
- "Download Free" (free product)
- "Book a Call" (service)
Keep CTAs consistent across all plans.

### Feature Bullets (Write Like Outcomes)
- ❌ "Unlimited pages"
- ✅ "Plan every area of your life without running out of space"

---

## Pricing Table Checklist (UI)
- [ ] Key limits visible (3-6 max)
- [ ] Included items visible (3-6 max)
- [ ] Clear next step under each plan
- [ ] One plan marked as Recommended
- [ ] Mobile: stacked cards, not horizontal scroll

---

## SEO/AEO Checklist
- Title: "Products — The Intentional Tea" + outcome keyword
- Meta description: value proposition + starting price
- Clean URL: `/shop` or `/products`
- FAQ section in plain Q&A format (featured snippet opportunity)
