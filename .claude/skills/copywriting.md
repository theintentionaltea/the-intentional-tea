# Copywriting Guide

Expert conversion copywriting focused on creating clear, compelling marketing copy that drives action.

## Core Principles

- **Clarity trumps cleverness** — prioritize understanding over creative flourish
- **Benefits over features** — focus on customer outcomes rather than specifications
- **Specificity over vagueness** — use concrete examples instead of abstract language
- **Customer language** — mirror how your audience actually speaks about their problems

---

## Writing Style Standards

- Simple: use words your reader uses in real conversation
- Specific: "saves 4 hours a week" beats "saves time"
- Active: subject does the action
- Confident: no hedging language
- Honest: no unsubstantiated claims

Remove: jargon, passive constructions, exclamation points, buzzwords without substance.

---

## Page Structure Framework

Effective pages follow this hierarchy:

1. **Headline** — communicates core value proposition
2. **Subheadline** — adds specificity, names who it's for
3. **Primary CTA** — above the fold
4. **Social proof** — logos, numbers, quotes
5. **Problem** — name the pain clearly
6. **Solution** — how it works
7. **Implementation** — what happens next
8. **Objections** — address the top 3 reasons not to buy
9. **Final CTA** — repeat, with different framing

---

## CTA Best Practices

Formula: **[Action Verb] + [What They Get]**

| Weak | Strong |
|---|---|
| Learn More | Get the Full Guide |
| Submit | Start My Free Trial |
| Click Here | Download the Template |
| Sign Up | Join 5,000 Planners |
| Buy Now | Get the Life Planner |

---

## Headline Formulas

- **Outcome-focused:** "Finally, a planner that works the way you think"
- **Specificity:** "Plan your entire year in one afternoon"
- **Contrast:** "Built for people who hate rigid systems"
- **Direct:** "The planner designed around your actual life"

---

## Benefit Bullets Format

**[Benefit]** — [specific proof or detail]

Example:
- **Flexible structure** — fill in what matters, skip what doesn't. No wasted pages.
- **Monthly + weekly views** — zoom out to plan big, zoom in to execute.

---

## Output Checklist

Before delivering copy, confirm:
- [ ] First sentence makes a real claim
- [ ] Benefit is stated before the feature
- [ ] CTA uses an action verb + what they get
- [ ] No jargon the reader wouldn't use themselves
- [ ] Proof exists near every bold claim
- [ ] Objections are addressed before the final CTA
