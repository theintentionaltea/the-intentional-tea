# Landing Page (High-Conversion)

A landing page is not a homepage. A homepage serves multiple intents. A landing page wins one intent: **one offer → one audience → one primary action**.

---

## Before You Design/Write

Gather (ask if missing):

1. **Page purpose** — what is the ONE primary action? (buy, download, signup, waitlist)
2. **Audience + context** — who, what problem, top 3 objections, traffic source, what do they already know?
3. **Proof + assets** — logos, testimonials, numbers, guarantees
4. **Constraints** — brand voice, design direction, mobile priority

---

## Core Structure

### Above the Fold (Must)
1. **Headline** (outcome + audience)
2. **Subheadline** (clarifies "how" + adds specificity)
3. **Primary CTA** (clear verb + what they get)
4. **One proof signal** (stat, short testimonial, or logo)
5. **Hero visual** (product screenshot or strong illustration)

### Mid Page (Argument)
6. **Problem → solution** (1 section)
7. **Benefits** (3-5, outcome-driven)
8. **How it works** (3 steps)
9. **Social proof** (testimonials or case study)

### Bottom (Objection Handling)
10. **FAQ** (6-12 Q&A)
11. **Risk reversal** (trial, cancel anytime, guarantee)
12. **Final CTA** (same verb as top)

---

## Layout Types

### A) Classic Hero + Sections
Best for: product that's understandable with a hero image

### B) Long-Form Story (Sales Page)
Best for: need to educate + overcome skepticism before buying

### C) Minimal Conversion Page
Best for: high-intent traffic (email list → known audience); short offer (download, waitlist)

### D) Comparison Landing Page
Best for: search intent includes alternatives ("X vs Y", "best for...")

---

## High-Conversion Strategies

### 1. Match Message to Traffic Source
If traffic is from Instagram, mirror the Instagram content's headline. Same promise, same visual tone.

### 2. Make the Next Step Obvious
One primary CTA. Avoid multiple competing CTAs above the fold.

### 3. Write Benefit-First
- Features: what it does
- Benefits: what that means for them

### 4. Use Specificity
- ❌ "Save time and streamline your life"
- ✅ "Plan your entire month in one Sunday afternoon"

### 5. Reduce Risk
Pick at least one:
- Free trial / free tier
- Money-back guarantee
- No credit card required
- Cancel anytime

### 6. Objection Handling Is a Section, Not a Footer
Add FAQ earlier if it's a high-friction offer. Put proof right next to the claim it supports.

## Copywriting Templates

### Headline Formulas
- "{Outcome} without {pain}"
- "The {category} for {specific audience}"
- "Finally, {benefit} that {addresses the frustration}"

### CTA Rules
- Verb + what they get
- ❌ "Learn more," "Submit"
- ✅ "Get the Planner," "Start My Reset," "Download Free"

### Benefit Bullets Format
**[Benefit]** — [proof or detail]

---

## SEO Checklist (When Relevant)

**Don't index when:** ad-only campaign, highly time-bound offer → use `noindex`

**Do index when:** evergreen offer, search intent matches the promise → add title, meta, internal links, FAQ schema
