# Content Strategy

Plan content that drives traffic, builds authority, and generates leads.

## Core Principle

Content must be **searchable, shareable, or both** — with search traffic as the foundation.
- **Searchable content** captures existing demand
- **Shareable content** creates demand through novel insights

---

## Content Types

| Type | Format |
|---|---|
| Searchable | Use-case content, hub-and-spoke structures, template libraries, how-to guides |
| Shareable | Thought leadership, data-driven pieces, expert roundups, case studies, personal stories |

---

## Strategic Framework

### Content Pillars
Identify 3-5 core topics aligned with:
- Products you sell
- Audience needs
- Search volume
- Competitor gaps

Each pillar spawns related subtopic clusters.

**For The Intentional Tea — example pillars:**
1. Intentional planning and digital organization
2. Life alignment and self-trust practices
3. Business operations for small business owners
4. Digital product reviews and recommendations
5. Intentional living rituals and routines

### Buyer Journey Mapping
Align keywords to journey stage:

| Stage | Modifier examples |
|---|---|
| Awareness | "what is," "how to," "why do I" |
| Consideration | "best," "vs," "alternatives," "review" |
| Decision | "pricing," "buy," "download," "get" |
| Implementation | "how to use," "tutorial," "examples" |

### Ideation Sources
- Keyword research (search volume + intent)
- Sales and DM transcripts (what people actually ask)
- Forum questions (Reddit, Facebook groups)
- Competitor content gaps
- Support / FAQ questions
- Comments on your own content

---

## Prioritization Scoring

Score ideas on:
- Customer impact (40%)
- Content-market fit (30%) — can you create the best version of this?
- Search potential (20%)
- Resource requirements (10%)

---

## Output Deliverables

For any content strategy session, produce:
1. Content pillars with rationale
2. Prioritized topic list with keywords and buyer journey stage
3. Topic cluster map showing how pieces interconnect
