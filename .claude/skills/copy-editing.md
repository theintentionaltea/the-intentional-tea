# Copy Editing Skill

Systematic framework for improving existing marketing copy through seven sequential editing passes.

## Core Approach

Good copy editing isn't about rewriting — it's about enhancing. Each focused pass targets one dimension, preventing issues that arise when attempting multiple corrections simultaneously.

---

## The Seven Sweeps

Run these in order. After completing all seven, loop back to verify earlier passes remain intact.

### Sweep 1: Clarity
**Goal:** Every sentence is immediately understood on first read.
- Remove any sentence that requires a second read
- Replace vague nouns with specific ones
- Cut sentences longer than 25 words into two

### Sweep 2: Voice and Tone
**Goal:** Consistent brand personality throughout.
- Does every paragraph sound like the same person?
- Flag anything that suddenly sounds formal when the rest is casual (or vice versa)
- Preserve the author's voice — don't homogenize into generic AI copy

### Sweep 3: "So What"
**Goal:** Every feature is connected to a benefit the reader cares about.
- For every "it has X" find the "which means you can Y"
- If you can't find the "so what" for a feature, cut it

### Sweep 4: "Prove It"
**Goal:** Every claim has evidence nearby.
- Identify all unsubstantiated claims: "powerful," "easy," "the best"
- Add: numbers, testimonials, before/after, specific outcomes
- If no proof exists, soften the claim or cut it

### Sweep 5: Specificity
**Goal:** Replace all vague language with concrete details.
- ❌ "save time" → ✅ "save 3 hours a week"
- ❌ "works for everyone" → ✅ "works for founders who already have clients but no system"
- ❌ "comprehensive" → ✅ list what's actually included

### Sweep 6: Heightened Emotion
**Goal:** Create emotional resonance — make the reader feel something.
- Find the moment in the copy where the reader should feel understood
- Sharpen the pain point: is it named precisely?
- Sharpen the relief: does the copy let the reader visualize the after?

### Sweep 7: Zero Risk
**Goal:** Remove every barrier near calls to action.
- What objections exist at the moment of clicking?
- Add nearby: guarantee, refund policy, "cancel anytime," testimonials, FAQ
- Make the next step feel safe

## Word-Level Optimizations

| Remove | Replace with |
|---|---|
| very, really, quite | cut entirely |
| utilize | use |
| in order to | to |
| at this point in time | now |
| due to the fact that | because |
| it is important to note | note (or cut) |

---

## Collaboration Rules

- Preserve the author's voice — don't make it sound like you wrote it
- Flag edits that change meaning, don't make them silently
- Deliver with annotations on why each edit improves the copy
