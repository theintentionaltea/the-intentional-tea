## CORE POSITIONING — READ THIS BEFORE WRITING ANYTHING BRAND-LEVEL

**The north star statement:**
> The Intentional Tea helps people create a life and business that actually fits who they are.

**What changed:** The brand used to center "twelve years of healing and inner work" as its primary credibility source and framing device. That framing is retired. It can still surface as a chapter in her story — but it is no longer the container the brand sits inside. The container now is **alignment and fit**, not healing and transformation.

**One audience, not two:** There is one audience: people whose life and work feel slightly forced, misaligned, or harder than they need to be. That shows up in two domains — how they live, and how they build — but it's the same person, the same root need, viewed from two angles. Don't write copy that splits the audience into two groups.

**The working model:**
- **Personal Alignment** — how someone lives. Boundaries, self-trust, routines that match actual energy, cycle awareness, astrology as a reflection tool, lifestyle design.
- **Business Alignment** — how someone builds. Systems, Notion, websites, workflows, project management, execution structure.
- **Tools & Resources** — the monetization layer underneath both. Not a third audience segment — this is what people buy once they're bought into the philosophy above.

**Tiara's role, reframed:** Not a healer, not a coach, not a "transformation guide." She's a translator — someone who identifies where a life or business doesn't match how a person actually functions, and converts that mismatch into usable structure. Her authority comes from pattern recognition and structural translation, backed by the Certified PM credential.

**Language filter — apply to every piece of brand-level copy:**

Allowed: alignment, fit, structure that works for you, self-trust in practice, reducing friction, clarity through structure, simplifying execution, "a life and business that fits."

Not allowed: healing as the main identity, "transform your life," therapy-coded framing, vague optimization language, "unlock your potential," "12 years of healing/trauma" as the lead credibility claim, casting the brand as two separate audiences.

**Current name: "Business Operations."** Older material used "Business Elevation" and "Business Operations Services" — both retired. (Not fully confirmed as the final final name — if in doubt on a customer-facing document, confirm before publishing.)

**What it is:** Operational infrastructure builds for small businesses — websites, systems, automations, and backend structure for owners who are done running everything manually.

**Current live offers (verified against theintentionaltea.com/book):**

**Clarity & Audit — $400 flat rate**
One intake, one focused session, one written document. A clear picture of where the business stands and what to address first, whether Tiara builds it or the client does. Fee credits toward any build booked within 30 days.

**Infrastructure Build — starting at $2,500**
The full build, scoped to the business. Website, systems, automations, digital infrastructure. Investment scales with complexity, confirmed at intake.

**Multi-Entity Build — starting at $5,000**
One backend for every moving part — multiple brands, locations, or complex digital launch systems. Scope and investment confirmed at intake.

**Ongoing Support — add-on, available with any build**
Updates, maintenance, new integrations, automation support after the build. Priority 48-hour response. No published pricing — scoped per client.

Note: "Tier" language (Tier 01/02/03) has been fully dropped from customer-facing copy. Don't reintroduce it.

**The process (live, 5 steps, deposit-first):**
1. Deposit locks the spot — pay to secure a start date, no calendar access before payment
2. Intake form — detailed, so Tiara can learn the business before touching anything
3. Kickoff + build — kickoff call, then research, setup, infrastructure, with check-ins at key milestones
4. Review + revisions — client reviews everything, requests changes
5. Handoff + support — recorded walkthrough of everything built; Ongoing Support available after

**Approach positioning (use as a model for tone):**
Most web designers hand over a beautiful site with no plan for what happens when leads come in. Most operations people can't design their way out of a template. Tiara does both — front end through fulfillment. Clients already have the thing: a real service, real customers, real experience. What's missing is the structure around it.

**First clients:** Family businesses (a snowball stand, her father's business), with referral clients now entering the pipeline. Useful as real, specific case-study material.
